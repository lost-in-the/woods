- Request flows now include the calls in an `if`, `unless`, ternary or `case` predicate, emitted ahead of the conditional at its own level. `if @widget.save` is the side effect of a typical create action, and dropping it left an action's flow with only the conditional and its responses. Calls inside branches, blocks and transactions were already reported; calls nested in another call's arguments and `.new` remain excluded by design, with enqueues in arguments still reported.
