- Console `tools/list` no longer advertises a top-level `oneOf` on `console_find` or a top-level `allOf` on `console_aggregate`; some MCP clients reject or mishandle a tool schema with a root combinator. The advertised `console_find` `by` property also drops `minProperties`. Everything else in both schemas is unchanged, and their descriptions now state the rules: exactly one of `id` or a non-empty `by` for `console_find`, and `column` for `sum`, `average`, `minimum` and `maximum`. The Console still refuses a call that breaks either rule, using the full schema and before any query runs, but the refusal now comes from the Console's dispatch rather than the SDK's pre-check. Compatibility fix only: no failure was reproduced on current Claude clients.
  - **Refusals with `code`, `argument` and `hint` (Console dispatch):** a `console_find` call with both `id` and `by`, with neither, or with an empty `by` (`error_code: invalid_locator`, `argument: id|by`), and a `console_aggregate` non-count call without `column` (`error_code: column_required`, `argument: column`). The text still starts with `Invalid arguments:`, now followed by a `Hint:` line, and `_meta` carries `error_code`, `tool`, `argument` and `hint`.
  - **Refusals unchanged (SDK pre-check, text only, no `_meta`):** a missing required argument (`Missing required arguments: ...`) and any other schema failure, such as a wrong type, a bad pattern, an out-of-range integer or an unknown `function`.
  - **Table gate first:** a call on a blocked model gets the table refusal even when its arguments also break one of the two rules.
  - **Embedders:** `EmbeddedExecutor#send_request` returns `code`, `argument` and `hint` beside `error` on the two rule refusals, and `code: invalid_arguments` or `missing_required_arguments` on other schema refusals. `CONTRACT_MATRIX` rows gain `arguments.advertised_constraints` and `combination_refusal`.
