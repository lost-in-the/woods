- Console `tools/list` no longer advertises a top-level `oneOf` on `console_find` or a top-level `allOf` on `console_aggregate`; some MCP clients reject or mishandle a tool schema with a root combinator. Every other part of both schemas is unchanged, and their descriptions now state the rules: exactly one of `id` or a non-empty `by` for `console_find`, and `column` for `sum`, `average`, `minimum` and `maximum`. The Console still refuses a call that breaks either rule, with the full schema and before any query runs, but the refusal now comes from the Console's dispatch rather than the SDK's pre-check. Its text still starts with `Invalid arguments:`, now followed by a `Hint:` line, and its `_meta` carries `error_code` (`invalid_locator` or `column_required`), `tool`, `argument` and `hint`. An embedder reading `EmbeddedExecutor#send_request` directly sees `code`, `argument` and `hint` beside `error` on those refusals, and `code: invalid_arguments` or `missing_required_arguments` on other schema refusals. `CONTRACT_MATRIX` rows gain `arguments.advertised_constraints` and `combination_refusal`. Compatibility fix only: no failure was reproduced on current Claude clients.
