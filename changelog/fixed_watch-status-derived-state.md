- A watch daemon that died without clearing its record no longer reports `state: running` beside `alive: false`. `woods:watch_status` and the `woods_status` MCP tool now derive `state` from liveness at read time: `dead` when the record names a same-host pid that no longer exists, `stale` when it is older than `stale_after_seconds`, and `running` or `degraded` only while the daemon is alive. The daemon's own last word is kept as `recorded_state` (`Status#report`).
