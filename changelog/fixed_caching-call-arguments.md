- `CachingExtractor` reads each cache call's own arguments with Prism, so `metadata.cache_calls[].key_pattern` is now filled for ERB, HAML, and jbuilder fragment caches (`cache [record, "v2"] do`, `json.cache! [widget, 'v2'] do`), not only for `Rails.cache.*`. Keys keep interpolation, brackets, and multi-line arguments whole (up to 120 characters), and `cache_if`/`cache_unless`/`cache_if!` take the key after the condition. A new `options` field records literal `expires_in`, `race_condition_ttl`, `if`, and `unless` values. `ttl` no longer carries a trailing ` do` or loses a closing parenthesis. ERB and HAML `cache_if`/`cache_unless` blocks are now detected as fragment caching.
