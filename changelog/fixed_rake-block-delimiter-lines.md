- A rake task body now keeps a statement that shares a line with the block's `do`/`{` or `end`/`}` (`task backfill: :environment do ::Tenant.where(active: true).find_each(&:touch)` followed by `end`). The body used to be only the lines strictly between the two delimiters, so such a statement reached no dependency scan and the task carried no edge to the model, service or job it named; `source_lines` counted it as 0. The leading `::` was never the trigger (LOS-129).
