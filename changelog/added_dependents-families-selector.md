- `dependents` takes an optional `families:` selector and reports detailed categories. Every row but the root now also carries `category`, one of `application_code`, `rake_tasks`, `test_files`, `factories`, `schema_objects`, `migrations`, `documentation`, `agent_instructions` or `other`, each inside one of the five families, and `dependent_counts.categories` counts them over the whole traversal; the five family fields and the headline keep their meaning. A `doc` is `agent_instructions` when its path or a published alias path is an `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `CLAUDE.local.md`, `GEMINI.md` or `SKILL.md` file, lies under `.claude/` or `.github/instructions/`, or is `.github/copilot-instructions.md`; its text is never read. `families:` takes family or category names and lists only matching rows, chosen after the walk: unlike `types` it never prunes the traversal, so a selected row reached through an unselected one is still listed, marked `reached_through_hidden`, and `deps` keep only edges between listed rows (`hidden_deps` counts the rest). Paging applies to the selected rows. `dependent_counts` still covers every traversed row; the new `selection.counts` covers the selected ones, with lower bounds after a budget cutoff. With `explain`, unselected ancestors stay as context witnesses marked `selection: "not_selected"`. An empty or unknown selector is refused. Without `families:` the rows, their order and every existing field are unchanged.
