- Controller filters now record where they were declared, and the before filters in a request flow report that line and file. `metadata[:filters]` entries gain `line` and `file` when the site can be known: a proc filter's own source location, or the first `before_action`, `around_action` or `after_action` declaration of that kind (including the `prepend_`/`append_` forms) naming the filter in the controller or an app-defined ancestor, in method resolution order. A filter no app source declares, such as one a gem adds, keeps no site rather than a guessed one.
