- The first `woods_status` no longer waits on the RubyGems update probe. A missing or expired cache entry is answered at once from the last cached result and the probe runs once in a bounded background thread. `server.update` carries `pending: true` until the probe lands, then the usual `checked` and `latest_version`. No new network requests are made, and `WOODS_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1` still disables the probe.
