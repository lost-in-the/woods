- Lexical retrieval tokenizes faster and builds its corpus with fewer allocations, with identical terms. The tokenizer scans for runs of letters and digits first and splits only the runs that hold an upper-case letter at their CamelCase boundaries, through ASCII patterns when the text is ASCII (10-19% less CPU over the self-map's 44,152 field values, checked term-for-term against the former whole-text splitting). The index reader opens each published unit through a String path and a single `lstat` instead of `Pathname#join` plus two stat calls, a third of a corpus scan's allocations; a cold lexical build of the 11,038-unit self-map went from 8.0 s to 6.4 s.
