- `incremental_blast_radius_depth` now defaults to `:structural` (#946): an incremental run follows inheritance, include and extend edges to any depth and every other edge one hop, instead of the unbounded transitive closure. On a production app of about 11,400 units, a one-line non-leaf edit re-derived 440 units instead of 7,243 and took about half the time, with payloads equal to a full extraction on every change measured, including an STI root change that a depth of 1 got wrong. Nested `has_many :through` chains are reached through their `through_chain` edges, and flows within the flow assembler's depth are reassembled whatever the bound (#963). Set `incremental_blast_radius_depth = nil` to keep the previous unbounded closure, for example on a tree whose class hierarchy the graph does not record as inheritance edges.
