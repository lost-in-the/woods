- A component constructed outside a render call is a dependency (LOS-123). `Wrapper.new(primary: Kit::Button.new(label: "x"))`, a component passed as a constant, returned, kept as a hash value or tested with `is_a?` now links the caller to that component with a `code_reference` edge, verified by the constant-reference pass. Components are callers of components by default (`source_reference_callers`), and the pass adds no `code_reference` beside a `render` or `slot` edge to the same component, from a component or a controller, so a rendered component is linked once. Every occurrence stays a `metadata.references` span. On the field-test application, 57 of 96 constant-form callers of a UI-kit button had no edge.
