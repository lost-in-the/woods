- A direct `Extractor#refresh`, `#extract_changed` or `#extract_all` call no longer publishes over a generation another writer published while it ran. Such a call builds its stage without the lock and takes the lock only to publish; it used to renumber its stage to the generation after the current one, so a stage seeded from generation 1 could go live as generation 3 without generation 2's changes. It now keeps the number it staged under and refuses with `generation moved to <n> during publication (expected <m>)`, removing its stage and leaving the newer generation active. Rake tasks, the watch daemon, the MCP operator tools and `Woods.extract!` hold the lock for the whole run and are unaffected.
