- `config.precompute_flows = :best_effort` (W-14, #764). The run publishes every flow that assembles and does not abort. An action whose flow fails to assemble is left out of `flows/flow_index.json` and of the controller's `flow_paths`, and is recorded under `flow_failures` in `skipped_files.json` with `entry_point`, `controller`, `action`, `reason: flow_assembly_error` and `error`. Full and incremental runs record the same failures. `true` keeps its fail-closed contract and `false` stays the default. `precompute_flows` now rejects values other than `true`, `false` and `:best_effort`.
- `Woods::MCP::IndexReader#flow_failures` returns the pinned generation's best-effort flow failures (W-14) for readers such as Woods Map. It is empty when there are none or when the generation predates `skipped_files.json`.
