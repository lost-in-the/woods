- A writer killed mid-run (a container restart, `SIGKILL`, an OOM kill) no longer blocks the watcher and every other writer until `LOCK_STALE_TIMEOUT` passes. `extraction.lock` now records the holder's host beside its pid, and a lock written on this host by a pid that no longer exists is stale at once. Locks from another host, or written before the host was recorded, are still judged by age, since a pid cannot be checked across namespaces.
