- Lexical `codebase_retrieve` ranks the unit that answers more of a question above units that repeat one rare word: terms are singularized (a question about "labels" reaches `set_label`), and a document's score is weighted by the share of the query's words it holds. In full evidence, one long top source no longer takes the whole budget and ends assembly: each source gets a fair share among the next four candidates, so a lexical answer keeps several sources.
