- Embedding dump retention (`dump_retention_count`) now counts only dumps that were promoted to `dumps/latest`. A dump left behind by a run that failed or was killed before promotion used to take a place in the window, so a few leftovers could delete promoted dumps early. Each promoted dump now carries a `.promoted` marker; a dump without one is kept out of the count and removed only after it has sat untouched for 24 hours, so a writer still dumping in another process never loses its directory. Dumps written by an earlier Woods (the unmarked ones older than the first marked dump) are marked once, the first time retention runs, and `dumps/.promoted_markers_migrated` records that this happened. The list of dumps to mark is written first (`dumps/.promoted_markers_migrating`), so a migration that fails or is interrupted partway resumes with the same dumps. While any of them is less than 24 hours old the migration waits and nothing is pruned, since a fresh unmarked dump may be another writer's still being written.
