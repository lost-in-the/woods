- `woods_status` reports `worktrees` as a fixed-size summary by default: `{ scope: "other_checkouts", count:, by_state: { indexed:, unextracted:, missing:, unreadable: } }`, counting the linked checkouts other than the one the call describes (the launch checkout, or the one named by `root`). A machine with dozens of worktrees no longer pays one entry per checkout on every status call. The new `worktrees` argument takes `summary` (default), `list` (the previous per-checkout entries, served checkout included) or `none` (section omitted).
