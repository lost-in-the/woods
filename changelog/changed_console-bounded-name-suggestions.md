- Console refusals for an unknown model or column no longer list every known name. They name at most 5 near matches, ordered by closeness and then name, with a count of known names (`Unknown model: Usr. Did you mean: User? (412 known models)`), or `No close match among N known models.` when nothing is close. Models on `console_blocked_tables` and redacted columns are never suggested or counted. Validation and authorization are unchanged.
