- `woods_status` reports `index.history.state` (`current`, `stale` or `unknown`) by comparing the HEAD recorded in the published generation with the live HEAD. A commit with no source change now shows history as stale even when source freshness is current. The live HEAD resolves through `Woods::GitProvenance`, so linked worktrees and `WOODS_GIT_DIR` behave as they do during extraction; a directory Git cannot resolve is `unknown`, never stale.
