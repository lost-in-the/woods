- Template detection outside `ViewTemplateExtractor` now recognizes HAML and jbuilder as well as ERB. `CachingExtractor` scans `.haml` and `.jbuilder` views (and recognizes jbuilder `json.cache!` blocks), and incremental runs dispatch those views to it. `MailerExtractor` records `.haml` and `.jbuilder` mailer templates, and `ViewComponentExtractor` detects HAML, jbuilder, and Slim sidecars in the sidecar-directory form. The shared list lives in `Woods::Extractors::TemplateExtensions`, derived from `ViewTemplateExtractor::ENGINES`.
