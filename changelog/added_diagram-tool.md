- The Index MCP server has an always-on `diagram` tool, and `woods:diagram[kind,identifier]` prints the same text (#826). It answers with Mermaid text for one unit, built on demand from the pinned published generation and cached per generation; nothing is computed at extraction.
  - **Kinds:** `neighbourhood` (typed edges as a flowchart), `schema` (a model's table and associations as an ER diagram), `lifecycle` (a model event's callback chain as a sequence diagram) and `impact` (the change impact report's flowchart for the unit as a planned change).
  - **Depth:** each kind defines its own. Neighbourhood graph hops 1–3 (default 1), schema association hops 1–3 (default 1), lifecycle 0–2 (0 is the event's own chain; 1 and 2 follow its `perform_later` and `deliver_later` hops into the jobs and mailers they reach, from flow data assembled over the published units) and impact reverse hops 0–10 (default 2).
  - **Budgets:** 150 units, 40 tables, 150 participants and messages, and the impact renderer's 60 nodes and 120 edges. A diagram over its budget is refused with its size (`over_budget`), never truncated. A depth outside its kind's range is `invalid_params`.
  - **GitHub:** every text parses with the Mermaid GitHub renders (11.16.1). `spec/diagrams/github_mermaid_spec.rb` checks this in CI with that build, vendored for the spec only.
  - `Woods::Diagrams` builds the text, and Woods Map's Graph, Schema and Lifecycle panels can draw from it. The neighbourhood, schema at depth 1 and lifecycle at depth 0 are the map's texts, byte for byte.
  - `Woods::Impact::Renderers.mermaid_renderer` and `Mermaid#truncated?` let a caller tell when the impact flowchart was cut at its display budget.
  - The Index Server defines 31 schemas; 15 register in the packaged default.
