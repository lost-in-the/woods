- Controller and mailer units now record `metadata.action_facts` (W-7, #757): for each action, the instance variables its body and its applicable before/around filters assign (attributed to the method and file), the params it reads (permitted-params calls marked `permitted: true`), what it renders (template, partial with locals, format, head, or the implicit template), where it redirects and the `respond_to` formats, each with its line. Read from the syntax tree, so comments and strings never contribute. Additive: `actions` stays a list of names.
