- Collect coverage in the Ruby 3.3 unit job instead of a second full suite run. Each parallel process stores its own SimpleCov resultset, and `script/parallel-rspec` merges them once and applies the unchanged 90% line floor. The SimpleCov settings now live in `spec/simplecov_profile.rb`. The `coverage` CI job stays as a thin check that fails when the unit jobs fail, so the release validator and maintenance rulesets keep their required check name.
