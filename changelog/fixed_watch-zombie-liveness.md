- A watch daemon that was killed but never reaped (a zombie, common in containers whose PID 1 does not reap orphans) no longer reads as alive. On Linux, a same-host pid whose `/proc/<pid>/status` reports `State: Z` is now dead, so `woods:watch_status` and `woods_status` report `state: dead` with `alive: false`, and a new `woods:watch` reclaims the zombie's status and startup claim instead of standing down. Other platforms, and a `/proc` that cannot be read, keep the signal-0 check.
