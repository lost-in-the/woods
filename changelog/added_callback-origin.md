- Model callbacks, mailer callbacks and controller filters (the unit's `filters` and each action chunk's) carry an `origin` beside `analysis_status`: `app` (defined under `Rails.root`, concerns included), `framework` (a Rails gem), `gem` (any other bundled gem or the standard library) or `unknown` (no source: a native method, a string filter, a method the class does not define). `analysis_status` says whether a body could be read; `origin` says who wrote it, so an application proc and a framework autosave callback no longer read alike. A symbol filter is placed by the owner of the method it names, a proc by its source location; nothing is called. Existing keys are unchanged; re-extract to publish the field.
