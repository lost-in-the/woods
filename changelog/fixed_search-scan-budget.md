- Deep-field `search` (`fields: ["source_code"]` or `["metadata"]`) no longer stops after a fixed 500 unit files. By default it reads every candidate unit in the requested types, so a mid-size index is searched completely, and stops early only past 256 MiB of unit JSON or 10 seconds. Before, a search over a ~11k-unit index read about 4% of it and answered 0 results that agents took as absence. `WOODS_SEARCH_MAX_SCAN` still overrides, now as an exact unit count that replaces both caps. A stopped scan's JSON `scan` reports `scanned_units`, `candidate_units`, `total_units`, `scanned_bytes`, the `budget` and `limit_hit`; its hint names how much was read and `WOODS_SEARCH_MAX_SCAN`, and says plainly that zero results is not evidence of absence. Completeness stays `partial`/`scan_budget`.
