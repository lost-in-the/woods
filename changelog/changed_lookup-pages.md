- A full `lookup` answer longer than 40,000 characters now comes in pages. A large model with its concerns inlined rendered to hundreds of thousands of characters (263,082 on one application), which could spend most of an agent's context on one unit. Nothing is dropped: the pages, in order, are the complete rendering, cut at line ends. Each page opens and closes with its position (`lookup page 2 of 7: characters 40001-79988 of 263082`) and says which `page` to ask for next; `_meta.lookup_page` carries the same as data. Set `WOODS_MCP_LOOKUP_MAX_CHARS` to change the limit (`0` turns paging off), or pass `max_chars` on a call. A unit that fits is answered exactly as before. `evidence: "compact"`/`"outline"` keep their own `budget`.
