- A full `lookup` answer longer than 40,000 characters now comes in pages. A large model with its concerns inlined rendered to hundreds of thousands of characters (263,082 on one application), which could spend most of an agent's context on one unit. Nothing is dropped: the pages, in order, are the complete rendering, cut at line ends. Each page opens and closes with its position (`lookup page 2 of 7: characters 40001-79988 of 263082`) and says which `page` and `answer_id` to ask for next; `_meta.lookup_page` carries the same as data. A later page must name the `answer_id` its predecessor gave and is refused with `stale_index` when the unit has changed since (the index was republished between two calls), so pages of two versions are never joined. Set `WOODS_MCP_LOOKUP_MAX_CHARS` to change the limit (`0` turns paging off), or pass `max_chars` on a call. In the JSON response format the unit itself is paged, so every page parses and holds a readable part of it: `{"lookup_page": {...}, "unit": {...}}`. A value that fits travels whole; long source is cut at line ends and a long list between items, and `lookup_page.parts` says which lines or items a page holds. Merging the pages' `unit` objects in order (objects by key; strings and lists under the same key joined) gives the unit. A unit that fits is answered exactly as before. `evidence: "compact"`/`"outline"` keep their own `budget`.
