- External services the application calls (#814). The Gemfile configuration unit records `metadata.external_services`: each catalogued service whose client gem is declared for runtime, such as Stripe, Amazon S3, Twilio or Sentry, with its category and gems. A unit whose source calls through a client constant (`Stripe::Charge.create`, `Aws::S3::Client.new`) gains an `external_service` dependency to that service (`via: service_call`), so `dependents` of the service lists its callers. Unknown gems and generic HTTP clients publish nothing. No URL or credential is read. See [External services](docs/EXTRACTOR_REFERENCE.md#external-services).
