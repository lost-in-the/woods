- A scoped lexical retrieval (`WOODS_RETRIEVAL_MODE=lexical` with `packages:`/`source_paths:`) no longer builds a second lexical index over the eligible records on every request. The pipeline's index, built once, now derives a view over the eligible keys: the BM25 statistics are recomputed over that subset and the tokenised documents are shared, so the answer is identical to the rebuilt index's while the per-request cost drops from re-tokenising every eligible unit (4.8 s for a 6,130-unit scope on the gem's own self-map) to the eligibility pass plus the search. `Retrieval::LexicalIndex#restricted_to(keys)` is the new public seam.
