- A Ruby edit no longer re-extracts every job and serializer (#890). `Extraction::HybridRerunPolicy` reads the published source-reference baseline and refreshes a hybrid family whole only when a changed file is one of its units' own sources, declares (before or after the edit) a constant one of its units names (resolved through the reference's lexical nesting and every prefix of the constant path), defines constants or methods by a construct the collector cannot see (`const_set`, `define_method`, `class_eval`, `Class.new` and the like), or when runtime discovery after the reload disagrees with the index on a class (a discovered class it does not hold, or a class-discovered unit discovery no longer finds); otherwise its units in the blast radius are re-extracted on their own, like every other family. Without a baseline, after a partial eager load, or for a unit source the baseline never analysed, every Ruby edit refreshes both families as before. The one shape this cannot see is a constant a changed file creates with none of those markers and without declaring it: a job or serializer whose runtime facts depend on it keeps them until the next full extraction or its own change. Each decision and its reason are logged.
