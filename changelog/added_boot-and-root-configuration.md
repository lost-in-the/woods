- Index the Ruby an application boots, seeds and builds from as `configuration` units (#678). On top of `config/initializers` and `config/environments`, the configuration extractor now covers `config/boot.rb` (`config_type` `boot`), `config/environment.rb` (`environment`), `config/importmap.rb` (`importmap`), `config/deploy.rb` and `config/deploy/**` (`deploy`), `db/seeds.rb` and `db/seeds/**` (`seeds`), and the root `Gemfile` (`gemfile`, with its declarations in `metadata.gems`) and `Rakefile` (`rakefile`). `config/routes.rb` stays with the route extractor and `config/application.rb` with the behavioral profile. Other Ruby directly under `config/` is not indexed.
