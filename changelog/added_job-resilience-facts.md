- Job units now publish their resilience facts (W-4, #754): `retry_on`, `discard_on`, `rescue_from`, `around_perform` and `rescue_handlers` (every handler in registration order, with `declared_in` and the literal `wait`, `attempts`, `queue`, `priority`, `jitter` and `block` options), plus `resilience_source`. With the job class loaded they come from the class itself (`rescue_handlers`, `_perform_callbacks` under the application root), so a declaration inherited from `ApplicationJob` or added by a concern counts and framework callbacks are left out; options are read from the handler's closure without calling it. Without the class they come from the class body's calls. Every key is present, so an empty `retry_on` states that the job does not retry.
