- Any unit whose source enqueues a job (`perform_later`, `perform_async`, `perform_in`, `perform_at`, or `.set(...)` before one) now records a `:job_enqueue` edge to that job, resolved through the lexical nesting at the call site, beside its existing `:code_reference` edge. Only job units recorded `:job_enqueue` before, so an enqueue written in a model or controller was indistinguishable from a plain mention of the job. `consolidate_dependencies` keeps a `:job_enqueue` edge beside the first edge to the same job.
