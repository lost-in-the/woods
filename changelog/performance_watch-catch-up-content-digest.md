- `woods:watch` start-up catch-up no longer re-extracts files whose modification time moved but whose content did not (#947). The modification time stays the first filter. Each candidate is then read once, bounded, and compared with the keyed digest every scope of the published `source_inputs.json` records for its path. A match drops the candidate, and the log says how many were dropped. Before, the comparison came from one whole-tree content scan. On a large application that scan could stop at its budget before it reached a candidate, so a reverted edit or a branch switch re-ran an extractor family wholesale and published nothing new. A path with no recorded digest, disagreeing digests, or bytes that cannot be read is still caught up.
