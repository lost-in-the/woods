- A Pundit policy's nested `Scope` class now ends at its own `end`. The scanner counted only `class`, `module` and `do` as block openers, so the `end` of a multi-line `def resolve` closed the Scope early: predicate methods later in the Scope were listed as the policy's `authorization_actions`, and a `private` inside the Scope hid the policy's own actions declared after it. The Scope's extent now comes from the parsed source, and source that does not parse falls back to a line scan that counts every block opener and ignores keywords in comments and strings.
