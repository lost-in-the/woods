- A routed action a controller inherits from a gem controller (an app sessions controller that subclasses an authentication engine's and adds no `create` of its own) is no longer reported as `missing_action` in `unresolvable_routes`. The controller unit records such actions in `metadata.inherited_gem_actions` with the gem owner, and the graph node carries their names; the gem bodies are still not admitted as actions, chunks, or flows.
