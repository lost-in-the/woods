- `frequency_human_readable` no longer misdescribes two cron shapes. A weekday list that repeats a day (`0 0 * * 0,7`, where 0 and 7 both mean Sunday) read "weekly on Sunday and Sunday"; each day is now named once. An hour step that does not divide the day (`0 */15 * * *`, which fires at 00:00 and 15:00, gaps of 15 and 9 hours) read "every 15 hours"; it now lists the hours it fires at, or falls back to the raw expression when there are too many to list.
