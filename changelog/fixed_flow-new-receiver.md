- Flow assembly now follows `Const.new.method` calls into the named unit. `PaymentProcessor.new.process(order)` and `Billing::Gateway.new.charge(order)` previously produced no service step, because the `.new` receiver did not match a constant. Flows from `woods:extract` with `precompute_flows` enabled, and `FlowAssembler` output generally, gain the steps for these services; flows for existing apps can therefore grow on the next extraction. The recorded operation target is unchanged.
