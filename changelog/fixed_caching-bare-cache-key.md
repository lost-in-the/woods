- `CachingExtractor` no longer reports a `cache_key` or `cache_version` call for a bare identifier, such as the local in `json.cache! cache_key do` or `<% cache cache_key do %>`. These methods now count only when called on a receiver (`record.cache_key`, `record.cache_key_with_version`, `record.cache_version`) or defined (`def cache_key`). `cache_key_with_version` on a receiver was previously missed.
