- `CachingExtractor` no longer reports a `cache_key` or `cache_version` call for a bare identifier passed to another cache call, such as the local in `json.cache! cache_key do`, `<% cache [cache_key, 'v1'] do %>`, or `Rails.cache.fetch(cache_key)`. A receiver call (`record.cache_key`) still counts there, and an implicit-self call anywhere else (`"#{cache_key}/x"` in a model) still counts. `cache_key_with_version` is now counted; the word boundary previously rejected it.
