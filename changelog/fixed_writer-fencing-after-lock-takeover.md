- A writer that resumes after its lock was taken over can no longer change a published generation (#1004). Every writer now stages its payload in a private `payloads/.stage-<n>-<token>` directory, so a takeover never deletes or reuses another writer's directory. Promoting the stage to `gen-<n>`, writing `generation.json` and pruning old payloads run only while the writer proves it still owns the lock, under the same guard a takeover takes. An obsolete writer stops at that point, removes its own stage and reports `Could not publish generation … no longer held by this writer`; the successor's payload, the current generation and pinned readers are unchanged.
