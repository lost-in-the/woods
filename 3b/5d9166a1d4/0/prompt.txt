## One difference from your prediction worth knowing

You expected absolute gem paths. On this app they come out **relative**:

  vendor/bundle/ruby/4.0.0/gems/activestorage-7.1.6/app/models/active_storage/blob.rb

Because BUNDLE_PATH is `/app/vendor/bundle`, i.e. the bundle is vendored *inside* Rails.root. Confirmed the files exist at those relative paths in the extraction environment, which is why validate is clean.

Two consequences you may want to think about, since vendoring into Rails.root is a common deployment shape:

1. Your "outside the app root" branches will not fire for these apps. The gem paths are inside Rails.root, so they are treated as ordinary in-root paths — they simply resolve. That is the right outcome here, but it means the gem-owned reporting path you added is not what is being exercised, and an app that vendors its bundle will never see it.
2. Anything reading the index from **outside** the extraction environment gets paths that do not resolve. In my case the index is written inside a container whose vendor/bundle is a Docker volume, so those 10 paths resolve in-container and do not exist on the host. Not a regression — 397266b's fabricated paths did not resolve anywhere — but a consumer reading dependency_graph.json or unit JSON off the host cannot open those files. If you want them portable, the gem root would have to be recorded separately from the relative remainder.
